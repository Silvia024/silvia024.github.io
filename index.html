Silvia Rossi, PhD

Senior Scientist at CWI, Amsterdam · Human Behaviour in Immersive Systems

Behavioural Analytics Machine Learning Social XR Experimental Design

I study how people navigate, experience and interact in immersive environments, and turn that behaviour into data that systems and designers can act on.

I am a Senior Scientist in the Distributed and Interactive Systems group at CWI, Amsterdam. I build end-to-end Python pipelines for multimodal behavioural data (e.g., movement, gaze, voice and decision timing), develop behavioural metrics and evaluation methods for interactive systems. I hold a PhD in Electronic and Electrical Engineering from UCL and have over seven years of experience in machine learning, predictive modelling and behavioural analytics.

What I bring

Behavioural analytics.

From raw multimodal logs to validated metrics: data pipelines, statistical analysis, information-theoretic measures.

Modelling & evaluation.

Predictive modelling, clustering and deep learning, with metric design and validation against reference results.

From studies to decisions.

User studies with single users and groups and translating their results into system and design choices; supervising PhD/MSc students and technical staff.
