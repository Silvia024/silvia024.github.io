Adaptive Immersive Streaming

Immersive systems must scale across heterogeneous users and remain within bandwidth and latency budgets — a challenge that grows harder as resolution, frame rate and degrees of freedom increase. This research puts the interactive user at the heart of the coding, streaming and rendering chain, rather than at the end of it.

By linking behavioural models to delivery decisions, we develop adaptive streaming strategies that are personalised, content-aware and application-dependent — enabling immersive experiences that are both high quality and network-efficient.
